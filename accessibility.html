Accessibility Statement

I build websites to be accessible to everyone, including this one.

My commitment

I aim for this site to meet WCAG 2.2 AA — things like proper colour contrast, full keyboard access, clear visible focus, real text labels on forms, and content that works with screen readers.

Honest limits

I test this site myself, but I'm one person and no site is perfect. If any part of it doesn't work for you, that's a barrier I want to remove — not something you should have to work around.

Tell me about a problem

Email hello@tannerwooddell.com or use the contact form and tell me what page you were on and what went wrong. I'll reply within 2 business days and fix barriers as quickly as I can.
