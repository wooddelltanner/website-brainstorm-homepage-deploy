I studied architectural design and worked for a couple of architects after college. I loved the design side of the work — figuring out how people move through a space and making it clear and easy to use. Since 2025, I've been building websites — studying user experience and design, and building with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It turned out to be the combination I'd been looking for: design that has to work for real people, every day.

What I care about most is delivering information in the clearest, most understood way. Your customers should never have to hunt for your hours, your menu, or your phone number. That's also why accessible design matters to me: a website only works if everyone can use it.

When you work with me, you get direct communication in plain English — no account managers, no jargon, no hand-offs. The same person who designs and builds your site is the person you talk to before launch and after it.

Fast-loading

Most visitors decide in a few seconds whether to stay on a website or leave — and a slow page makes that decision for them. If your site drags, people go back to the search results and call the next business instead.

That's why I build every site by hand with clean, lightweight code and properly sized images. There are no heavy page builders or bloated add-ons slowing things down, so your pages open quickly and your customers get straight to calling, booking, or visiting you.

Accessible to everyone

Your customers include people using screen readers, people navigating by keyboard, people with low vision, and plenty of people on old phones with cracked screens. If your site shuts any of them out, it shuts out paying customers.

I build accessibility in from the start: clear structure, readable text with strong contrast, buttons and forms that work with a keyboard, and descriptions for images. It makes the site easier for everyone to use — and easier to find, too.

Work directly with me

With an agency, you often talk to a salesperson, then an account manager, then someone else entirely builds your site. Details get lost at every hand-off, and you pay for all of those layers.

With me, there's one person: the one you talk to is the one designing and building your site, from the first conversation to launch day and beyond. You get premium quality and personal attention — without the agency overhead.
